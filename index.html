    About this prototype

    This map demonstrates a project-location profile using two real components. The thresholds are selected for this mockup; they are not adopted City standards.

    Benefits: job access

    Jobs reachable by walking and transit within 45 minutes. The estimate uses median travel times across departures in the July 13, 2026, 7–8:59 a.m. window, using a June 25, 2026 routing/transit snapshot and 2023 LODES jobs. Wage groups are breakdowns of total jobs, not additional scores.

    Burdens: modeled industrial air releases

    EPA RSEI 2022, model v2.3.12, toxicity-weighted air concentration (ToxConc). This covers modeled TRI-related air releases, not all pollution. It is a screening indicator, not a health-risk probability or a safe-exposure threshold. Absence of a flag does not establish safety.

    What the geography means

    Tracts are clipped to the municipal boundary, including Dog’s Head. City of Austin full- and limited-purpose limits. The extraterritorial jurisdiction (ETJ) is excluded. Boundary source. Tract access uses resident-worker weights with area or nearest-origin fallbacks; each profile identifies its method. RSEI is area weighted from EPA’s native 810 m grid. Tract averages can hide local variation.

    Address searches identify the containing H3 resolution 8 cell. Access is estimated from that cell’s origin, and RSEI is the mean over the full cell. These are neighborhood proxies, not parcel measurements. Available hexagons cover the reported share of the city area. Missing coverage stays unavailable. Tract lookup uses generalized Census boundaries and is approximate near edges.

    Reading the flags

    The combined map shows four combinations of the two flags: both, strong access only, elevated burden only, or neither. Missing either dimension remains unknown. Benefits and burdens remain separate; there is no net score or development recommendation. “Not flagged as strong” is not a low-access classification. Missing values are unknown. Other benefit and burden components are still under development. Classifications use unrounded values.
